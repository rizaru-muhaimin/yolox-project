The Process Innovation Section validates and implements innovative production lines using commercially available technologies. 
The Systems Development Section develops systems and software in-house and implements them. 
The Implementation Promotion Section then takes the lead in introducing the new technologies developed by these two sections into mass production.


The Smart Factory Concept
今までの人に頼った作業からより、新技術導入により安全で人にやさしい工場を目指します。
By introducing new technologies, we aim to reduce our reliance on manual work and create a safer, more worker-friendly factory.
目標値としては
・災害ゼロ、多様な人材開発、直行１/２、スペース１/２、リードタイム１/２、コスト３０％低減です。
Our targets are to achieve zero workplace accidents, enable a diverse workforce to thrive, cut direct labor, production space, and lead time in half, and reduce costs by 30%.

Here are some of the key initiatives undertaken by the Process Innovation Section. These include automated loading of stamped parts, automatic gap detection for instrument panel reinforcements, a system that detects welding spatter using current and voltage data, and an automated nut installation system.

プレス製品のピッキングは製品速度も速く難しい技術ですが
２Dカメラ、３Dカメラを駆使して部品整列とパレット詰めを作業として分けることによって自働化をトライ中となります。
Picking stamped parts is technically challenging because they move at high speed. 
To automate this process, we use both 2D and 3D cameras and divide the operation into two separate steps: aligning the parts and loading them onto pallets. We are currently testing this automated solution.

３台のロボットを強調制御し溶接することによって治具レスの取り組みです。
工場には補給部品のためにたくさんの段替え治具が保管されていますが、この技術が完成すればスペースに大きな影響を与えます。
This initiative aims to achieve fixtureless welding through the coordinated control of three robots. Our factories currently store a large number of changeover fixtures for service parts. Once this technology is fully developed, it could significantly reduce the amount of storage space required.


インパネリンフォースのレーザー溶接はしっかり溶接できているか？
１本数百打点の人でシムチェック行っております。
加工点でレーザー光の貫通＋３Dスキャンで板隙を検査することによって溶着されていることを検査しているシステムです


こちらは無人ナットラインとなります。
部品の入れ替えはAMRで行っています。
バラ積みされている製品を３Dスキャンでロボットがぶつからないように自動ピックし、ナット溶接後、完成品PLTに入れます。
付加価値が少ないナット付けをロボットに行わせることによって無人化してます。
This is our unmanned nut installation line. Parts are transported and replaced by an AMR. A 3D scanner detects the randomly stacked parts, allowing the robot to pick them automatically while avoiding collisions. After the nuts are welded, the finished parts are placed on a pallet. By assigning the low-value-added nut installation process to robots, we have achieved unmanned operation.

We are also working on automating the palletizing and depalletizing of TP boxes, as well as semi-automated equipment design using 3D CAD.

By building these capabilities in-house and deploying the system across our factories, we also aim to develop our IT talent.

Next, I’d like to discuss visual inspection. We are working with HIRAKIT, developed by TMC, as well as FutAiCa, which was developed in-house. Tanaka will provide more details later. In visual inspection, properly setting up the inspection environment and training the AI model are essential, regardless of which software is used. Our goal is not only to achieve results with the application we developed, but also to help users understand and learn the inspection process.

ヒューマノイドロボット（ルールベースによるTP箱仕分け/運搬動作

ヒューマノイドはまだまだ効果で量産でも投資対効果は望めませんが成長も著しい技術となってます。
将来を見据えて、TP箱の仕分けを題材に技術検証中です。
現在の実力値１２０秒に１個の仕分け
成功率９０％程度ですが、
最近はやりのフィジカルAIを導入することによりロバスト性があがるか検証段階です。

Humanoid robots are still expensive, making it difficult to achieve a sufficient return on investment in mass production. However, the technology is advancing rapidly. With future applications in mind, we are currently evaluating the technology through a trial involving the sorting of TP boxes. At present, the robot can sort one box every 120 seconds, with a success rate of approximately 90%. We are now testing whether the introduction of physical AI can improve the system’s robustness.
